What to do when you're bored

Everyone hits that moment — a free afternoon, friends over, or a quiet evening alone, and no idea what to actually do with it. This free activity generator fixes that in one tap. Spin it and get a random thing to do, matched to your mood: alone or with friends, indoors or outside, chilled out or full of energy.

There are over 100 hand-picked ideas in the mix, from quick five-minute distractions to activities that can fill a whole afternoon. No sign-up, no cost — just spin and go do it.

Bored at home alone?

Set the filter to Solo and get ideas built for one: learn a few guitar chords, try origami, start a tiny herb garden, run a home workout, or teach yourself a card trick. Small, satisfying things that turn a dull hour into something you actually enjoyed.

Bored with friends?

Switch to Friends for group-friendly ideas: charades, a blind taste test, a mini dance battle, a DIY escape-room challenge, or a mystery-ingredient cook-off. Perfect for sleepovers, hangouts, or breaking the "so… what do you want to do?" loop.

Indoor and outdoor ideas

Rainy day? Filter to Indoors for cosy, no-gear activities. Nice weather? Pick Outdoors for photo walks, geocaching, park games, stargazing, and more. You can also match your energy with the Chill and Active filters.
